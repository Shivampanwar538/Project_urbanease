About Urbanease

Simplifying urban living through trusted services and rentals

Our Story

Founded in 2025, Urbanease was born out of a simple idea: urban living should be effortless.

Our founders, experienced in both technology and urban development, saw an opportunity to create a platform that connects people with trusted service providers and quality rental items.

Our Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To simplify urban living by providing convenient access to trusted home services and quality rental items through a seamless digital platform.

Our Vision

To become the most trusted urban living platform in India, transforming how people access services and rentals in cities.

Our Core Values

These principles guide everything we do

Trust

We verify all service providers and rental items to ensure quality and reliability.

Convenience

We make urban living effortless with quick access to services and rentals.

Care

We treat every customer and partner with respect and understanding.

Innovation

We continuously improve our platform to better serve urban communities.
